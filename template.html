Honest note. This is not a brain simulation. The fly here is a plain run-and-tumble chemotaxis agent: it flies, smells the leaked delay, runs while the smell grows, and tumbles when it drops. The leak is real — the safe firmware freezes 50 ms per matching PIN digit — and that single side channel is enough for even a fly-grade searcher to win, far below the 10000 blind tries. The noise slider adds measurement jitter to that delay: watch how the fly still opens the safe but needs many more sniffs as the jitter grows, which is why noise alone is a weak defence. The PIN, the cipher key, and the decrypted volume are the real values recovered from the device.
