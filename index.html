Fly vs. CyberSafe

A searcher smells its way to the safe's PIN by following the leaked timing gradient — 50 ms per matching leading digit. Pick who searches, set the PIN, add measurement noise, and watch it fly.

Every cell is one PIN: column = first two digits, row = last two. Brighter = more leading digits correct (the leak). The searcher flies the continuous space and sniffs real PINs at the rounded cell.

Honest note. PIN = odour is a metaphor: the safe leaks a real gradient (50 ms per matching leading digit), and the searcher climbs it. Bacterium is a plain run-and-tumble reflex — even that cracks the code, and noise only makes it average more reads, not fail. Neural is a small spiking klinokinesis circuit, not the connectome. The full real MaleCNS 2026 connectome runs as a separate PC track (see the README). The PIN is editable here with no firmware change; the volume decrypt is real only for the device's own PIN 3952.
