Honest note. PIN = odour is a metaphor: the safe leaks a real gradient (50 ms per matching leading digit), and the searcher climbs it. Bacterium is a plain run-and-tumble reflex — even that cracks the code, and noise only makes it average more reads, not fail. Neural is a small spiking klinokinesis circuit, not the connectome.
